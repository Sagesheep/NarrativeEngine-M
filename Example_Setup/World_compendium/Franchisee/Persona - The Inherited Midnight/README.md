# Persona: The Inherited Midnight

A single long campaign across Persona 3, Persona 4 Golden, Persona 4 Arena, Persona 4 Arena Ultimax, Persona 4: Dancing All Night, Persona 5 Royal, and Persona 5 Strikers. A Kuzunoha case from Taisho 20 (1931 equivalent) connects the generations through Nanjo and Kirijo archives.

## Load into NarrativeEngine-M

1. Create a new campaign. Import `world_lore_persona.md` into World Lore.
2. Import `persona_campaign_rules.md` into Rules. It is a standalone Persona adaptation of the project's Immersive Mode principles; do not also load a complete competing GM ruleset.
3. Keep `campaign_director.md` as a separate GM reference. For automatic retrieval, import it into World Lore too. Its chunks are ordinary retrieval chunks, not always-included instructions. They contain endgame spoilers.
4. Paste the contents of the prompt block in `starter_prompt.md` into the campaign chat. The default opening is April 2009, with the historical prologue available first.

These are Markdown import files, not a campaign save or executable mod. No application code or existing campaign state is changed. Lore retrieval is not a permission system: GM secrets must be withheld in narration, and a user browsing the imported lore can read them.

The files use the mobile parser's explicit `[CHUNK: TYPE -- Title]` markers where needed and its structured character fields. Only two compact world-lore chunks and the rules kernel are always included; the director guide has no always-included chunks. The roster has no automatic wandering/location introductions. After import, review any engine-generated rumor pools: the app also derives candidates from all factions, locations and event summaries, including future-era entries. Keep those pools appropriate to the active year. Date and spoiler gates are narrative instructions, not software-enforced filters.

## Continuity choices

- Default: P3 male protagonist, Reload/FES-compatible history, The Answer / Episode Aigis; P4 Golden; Arena then Ultimax; Dancing All Night; P5 Royal; Strikers.
- Royal into Strikers is this campaign's explicit reconciliation. Royal-only allies remain real but have independent commitments; their absence is never a claim that Royal did not happen. Akechi's final status remains unresolved unless established in play.
- P3 Portable's female protagonist is an alternate starting continuity, not a second simultaneous P3 lead. Ask only if the player wants that route.
- P3/P5 Dancing and Q/Q2 may be optional Velvet Room interludes. P5 Tactica is an optional side chapter. None is required to understand the main campaign or secretly rewrites it.
- P5's unnamed calendar year is fixed here to 2016–2017 as a campaign convention. Dancing All Night is placed after Golden's summer epilogue in 2012 without asserting an exact day.
- Default player character: an original person who grows from 2009 to 2017. Established protagonists retain their own roles. Replacing a protagonist is an explicit alternative.

## Canon and original material

The Kirijo Group's former association with the Nanjo Group is an established series connection. **The specific 1931 joint case, its Nanjo financier and Kirijo engineer, the Three-Seal Ledger, the Custodians, and all later transfers of those case papers are original campaign material.** They do not establish a canon founding date, corporate split date, secret bloodline, or single cause behind every game's antagonist.

Raidou's setting is an alternate Taisho era. Treating its case archive as an ancestor of the modern Persona setting is a deliberate crossover premise. Raidou is a Kuzunoha title; the historical XIV is not automatically the modern holder. Demon summoning and Persona awakening remain distinct practices.
